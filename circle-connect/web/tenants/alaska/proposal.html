PRJ-01: SharePoint Estate Rationalization & Cold Storage Migration

Enterprise storage remediation across 4.48 TB, 29 site collections & 2,113,819 files

Fixed Professional Fee
$14,850 NZD
Recovers ~$13,139 / yr recurring

Alaska's live tenant audit revealed that over 790 GB of storage sits in completely stagnant historical repositories (Archive Contracts at 530 GB and Archive Tenders at 260 GB) where fewer than 20 files have been opened in the last 30 days. Microsoft bills this dormant capacity at premium active storage tiers, generating substantial ongoing overage penalties.

This flagship engagement transitions this vast data footprint into compliant, low-cost cold storage tiers, remediates excessive multi-version history bloat across 2.11 million files, and ensures 100% compliance with New Zealand construction retention statutes.

1

Estate Inventory & NZ Building Act Compliance Audit

Detailed cataloging of all 29 site collections and 2.11M files. Map historical project collateral against the New Zealand Building Act 2004 (10-year defect liability documentation, Producer Statements PS1–PS4, Code Compliance Certificates) and IRD 7-year financial retention rules.

2

Cold Target Architecture & Search Index Preservation

Architect Microsoft 365 Cold Archive and Azure Storage targets. Configure Microsoft Purview compliance indexing so that archived files remain instantly discoverable via search without requiring costly re-hydration to active storage. Preserve all metadata, timestamps, and permissions.

3

Automated Version Sprawl Cleanup & Quota Reclamation

Remediate multi-gigabyte version history bloat across high-churn CAD and specification libraries, safely reducing excessive 500-version histories to a standardized 50-version threshold. Purge orphaned upload fragments to deliver immediate active space relief.

4

Pilot Migration & Cryptographic Checksum Validation

Execute a 50 GB pilot migration across key construction formats (.dwg, .rvt, .pdf, .msg). Perform SHA-256 cryptographic hash checks before and after transfer to guarantee 100% bit-level data integrity with zero corruption. Validate on-demand file retrieval latency.

5

Production Archival Migration & Staged Cutover

Execute throttled, non-disruptive migration of Archive Contracts (530 GB / 537k files) and Archive Tenders (260 GB / 239k files). Transfer scheduled during off-peak windows to ensure zero disruption to live tenders. Configure clean read-only navigation shortcuts for seamless user access.

6

Continuous Lifecycle Automation & Project Manager SOP

Deploy automated Power Automate / Graph API workflows that automatically flag closed projects for cold archival after 12 months, preventing future bloat. Deliver the official Alaska Document Lifecycle Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) and post-migration audit report.

Secondary Scope (PRJ-02)

Exchange Quota Remediation & Inactive License Recovery

$7,850 NZD

Provides emergency quota intervention for key leads at 95–101% quota (Matthew Wade, Brian Phelan, Adam Olson) to prevent email bounce risks. Enables 1.5 TB Auto-Expanding Cloud Archiving and converts 43 inactive mailboxes to free Shared Mailboxes to reclaim paid licenses.

Secondary Scope (PRJ-03)

Project & Planner Modernization & License Rationalization

$9,800 NZD

Downsizes non-power users from expensive Project Plan 3 ($80+/mo) to standard Microsoft Planner, capturing ~$5,814/yr in direct licensing savings. Builds standardized Alaska Teams Planner boards (Procurement, Subcontractors, Health & Safety, Handover).
